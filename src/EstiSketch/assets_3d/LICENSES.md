- **Source / Author:** EstiSketch Clean-Room Stylized Architectural Asset Pack (`src/EstiSketch/assets_3d/manifest.json`)
- **Format:** glTF 2.0 Binary (`.glb`), centered at `bottom_center` (`[0, 0, 0]`) in Y-up meters, automatically scaled and rotated to each fixture's parametric footprint and height by `estisketch_3d_core::AssetLibrary`.
